About Paws & Hope Animal Rescue

Paws & Hope Animal Rescue is a non-profit organisation focused on helping animals in need and connecting them with responsible and caring families.

Our organisation aims to provide animals with care, protection and opportunities to find safe and loving permanent homes.

Our Mission

Our mission is to promote animal welfare by supporting rescued animals, encouraging responsible pet ownership and helping suitable animals find loving homes.

Our Vision

Our vision is to create a community where animals are treated with kindness and respect and where responsible adoption and animal welfare are valued.

Our Team

Rescue Team

Our rescue team assists with the care and support of animals that come into the organisation.

Adoption Team

Our adoption team helps potential adopters learn about the adoption process and find an animal that is suitable for their home and lifestyle.

Volunteer Team

Our volunteers assist with various activities that support the animals and the organisation.
