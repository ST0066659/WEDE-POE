Adoption & Services

Paws & Hope Animal Rescue provides services that support animals in need and encourage responsible pet ownership.

Animal Adoption

Adoption gives suitable rescued animals the opportunity to find permanent homes with responsible families.

Potential adopters should consider factors such as the animal's needs, their living environment, available time and their ability to provide long-term care.

Enquire About Adoption

Animal Rescue

Our rescue service focuses on supporting animals that need protection, care and assistance.

Rescued animals are assessed according to their individual needs and are provided with appropriate care while suitable homes are considered.

Foster Care

Foster care provides animals with temporary homes while they wait for adoption or other suitable arrangements.

Foster families can provide animals with a safe environment, daily care and attention while they are waiting for their next stage of care.

Enquire About Foster Care

Animal Education

Education can help people understand the responsibilities involved in caring for animals and making informed decisions about pet ownership and adoption.

We encourage members of the community to learn about responsible animal care and the importance of treating animals with kindness and respect.

Animal Welfare

Animal welfare involves considering the physical and behavioural needs of animals and providing appropriate care and protection.

Paws & Hope Animal Rescue aims to promote responsible treatment of animals and encourage community involvement in animal welfare.
